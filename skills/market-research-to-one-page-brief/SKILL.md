# Market research to one-page brief

This job runs on the user's own MyClawn cloud computer: a real browser that stays signed in, its own email address and files. It can outlast the chat and comes back with the answer, a screenshot of the final screen and a link to a screen recording.

## Setup

The user must have the MyClawn Computer connector signed in. If it is not, follow the setup in the cloud-computer-task skill of this repository and let the user do the sign-in; do not create accounts for them.

## Task

Hand this task to the connector's task tool, in the user's own words. The job page gives this example:

> Research the market for standing desks in Germany. In one page give: market size if a source states one, the five biggest sellers, typical price range, and two trends. Put a link after every claim and list what you could not find.

Replace the placeholders and the example values with the user's own. Show the final wording to the user before sending anything that submits a form, books something or writes to a third party.

## Steps

1. The job page says: “You name the market, the region and the questions you need answered.” Write the task with the goal, the sites, what the result must look like and what must not be done.
2. Send it with the connector's task tool and wait for the result.
3. What the computer does, as the job page describes it:
   - It searches and opens the sources in its own browser, and notes the figures and claims with their addresses.
   - It answers with a one-page brief where each claim has its source link; what it could not find is listed as not found.
4. Report the answer, the screenshot and the recording link.

## Limits

- It reads public sources. Paid reports behind a paywall are not opened unless you hold the login and sign in on the live screen.
- A figure is only as good as its source; it names the source so you can judge.
- It writes a brief to start from, not advice to base an investment on.
